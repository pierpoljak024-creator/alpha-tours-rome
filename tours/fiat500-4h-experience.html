Your 4-Hour Journey

Every moment is crafted to immerse you in authentic Roman culture — from iconic viewpoints to hidden gems, local flavours, and unforgettable surprises.

🚩 Circo Massimo — Limoncello & Welcome Photos

Your adventure begins at the legendary Circo Massimo, where you'll meet your guide and your iconic vintage Fiat 500. Kick off the experience with a refreshing limoncello toast — the perfect Italian welcome.

Capture the moment with professional-style photos of you and the Fiat 500 against the stunning backdrop of the Roseto Comunale (the Municipal Rose Garden) and the Palatine Hill. A picture-perfect start to your Roman adventure.

📸 Photo stop with Fiat 500 + Roseto Comunale backdrop

⛲ Fontana di Acqua Paola — Il Fontanone

Hop in the Fiat 500 and cruise up to one of Rome's most spectacular fountains — the Fontana di Acqua Paola, also known as "Il Fontanone". Located on the Janiculum Hill, this monumental Baroque fountain offers breathtaking views over the city.

Marvel at the majestic water jets and take in the panoramic scenery of Rome's rooftops stretching out below.

🌅 Gianicolo (Janiculum Hill) — Optional Photo Stop

Continue to the top of the Gianicolo (Janiculum Hill), one of Rome's most beloved viewpoints. If you wish, we'll stop here for a quick photo session capturing the unbeatable panoramic vista of the Eternal City — from St. Peter's Dome to the historic centre.

This stop is entirely optional, so you can soak in the views at your own pace.

📷 Optional photo stop — panoramic Rome views

🧺 Villa Pamphili — Picnic with Live Music

Escape the city bustle as we arrive at the lush Villa Pamphili, Rome's largest public park. Here, a delightful picnic awaits you under the shade of historic pines.

Savour a selection of fine Italian cheeses and cured meats, paired with fresh bread and seasonal accompaniments — a true taste of la dolce vita in nature's embrace. As you relax and enjoy, a live musical performance with songs will create an enchanting atmosphere, making this moment truly unforgettable.

🧀 Cheeses & cured meats picnic · 🎶 Live music & songs

🔭 The Optical Illusion of St. Peter's Basilica

Prepare to be amazed by one of Rome's most curious secrets — the optical illusion of St. Peter's Basilica. From a specific perspective along the avenue leading to the Vatican, the majestic dome appears to "shrink" or "move" as you approach it. Your guide will show you exactly where to stand to capture this mind-bending effect in your photos!

🔮 A must-see Roman curiosity + photo trick

🍓 Vincenzo Frutta e Verdura — Strawberries & Prosecco

We make a stop at the charming Vincenzo Frutta e Verdura stand, a beloved local spot. Here, depending on the season, you'll enjoy fresh strawberries or melon paired with a glass of sparkling prosecco — a simple yet exquisite Roman pleasure.

A Polaroid photo will be taken to capture this sweet, candid moment — a tangible memory to take home with you.

🥂 Strawberries/melon + prosecco · 📸 Polaroid photo keepsake

🍝 Testaccio — Roman Food Tasting at Taste Accio

We drive to the historic Testaccio neighbourhood — the true heart of Roman cuisine. At the renowned Taste Accio restaurant, you'll indulge in a tasting of authentic Roman foods. From classic pasta dishes to traditional Roman specialties, every bite tells a story of culinary heritage.

Testaccio is where locals go to eat, and this experience will give you a genuine taste of Roman gastronomic culture.

🍷 Authentic Roman food tasting at Taste Accio

🏁 Return to Circo Massimo

After an afternoon filled with iconic sights, hidden gems, live music, and unforgettable flavours, your guide will drive you back to Circo Massimo, where your 4-hour Dolce Vita journey began.

Arrivederci e grazie — until your next Roman adventure!
