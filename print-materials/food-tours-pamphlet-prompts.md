# Food Tours — Pamphlet Prompts for DALL-E

Use these prompts in ChatGPT with DALL-E to generate visual examples, then recreate in Canva for print.

---

## 🎯 PROMPT 1 — FRONT COVER

Design a front cover of a print travel pamphlet / flyer (portrait A5 / DL format) for a luxury tour company called "Alpha Tours Rome" promoting "Food Tours of Rome".

**Visual:** A wooden table in a Roman trattoria or market, photographed from a top-down angle making the food look abundant and inviting. The table takes up roughly the bottom two-thirds of the frame. On the table: plates of fresh pasta (cacio e pepe), a wooden board with cheeses and cured meats, a glass of red wine, pizza al taglio, and a small bowl of olive oil. In the soft-focus background (through a window): a glimpse of a charming Roman street with ivy-covered walls and warm golden light. The scene should feel warm, authentic, and "Eat Like a Roman!"

**Title (top third, large):** "FOOD TOURS" in a sophisticated serif font like Playfair Display, colour: forest green (#324e2c).

**Subtitle directly below the title, smaller:** "Eat Like a Roman" — same serif font, but in amber/gold (#f5b35d) with elegant letter-spacing and a subtle red (#a71b20) underline accent.

**Bottom edge (over a narrow semi-transparent band):** Logo "Alpha Tours Rome" in small caps. Tagline: "Hidden Trattorias · Local Specialties · Wine & Gelato" in tiny uppercase letters, dark charcoal.

**Colour palette (strict):** Cream/blush background (#fcefe6), forest green (#324e2c) for title, deep red (#a71b20) for accent elements (wine, tomatoes in food), amber/gold (#f5b35d) for subtitle. Clean, warm, "Italian kitchen" mood.

## 🎯 PROMPT 2 — BACK COVER

Design the back cover of a print travel pamphlet / flyer (portrait A5 / DL format) for "Alpha Tours Rome" — a luxury tour company — promoting their food tours.

**Visual (top half):** A soft collage with three warm vignette images fading into each other, all with golden-hour filter and soft blurred edges:
- Left panel: A food market stall (Mercato di Testaccio) with fresh produce, hanging salami, and a smiling vendor.
- Centre panel: Close-up of hands holding a supplì (fried rice ball) being pulled apart, revealing the mozzarella stretch.
- Right panel: A group of people walking through Trastevere's narrow streets at sunset, holding gelato cones.

**Layout — bottom half (cream/ivory background):**

Section title "OUR FOOD TOURS" in forest green (#324e2c), serif font, centred.

Below, the tours listed in a clean single column as small icon + text lines:

🍕 **Eat & Walk: Street Food Center & Trastevere** — 3h · Private
Taste supplì, pizza al taglio, and street food through Rome's most vibrant neighbourhoods.

🌟 **Guided Walking Food Tour in Testaccio** — 3h · Private · ★ Most Popular
Taste Roman cuisine from market stalls to hidden restaurants.

🍷 **Walking Food Tour with Tastings and Guide** — 3.5h · Small group
Sample wine, cheeses, pasta, pizza, gelato — the iconic Roman flavours.

**Left bottom corner:** Contact info: "📍 Various Meeting Points · ⏱ 3-3.5 hours · 📞 +39 375 829 7864" in tiny dark charcoal text.

**Right bottom corner (important):** A clearly visible QR code (square, about 2cm x 2cm) placed inside a thin rectangular border with a small label above it: "SCAN TO BOOK" in tiny uppercase letters, forest green colour. The QR code should link to the food tours page.

**Very bottom centre:** Small "Alpha Tours Rome" logo.

**Colour palette (strict):** Cream background (#fcefe6), forest green (#324e2c) for section titles, deep red (#a71b20) for tiny decorative accents and food descriptors, dark charcoal (#1a1a1a) for body text, amber/gold (#f5b35d) for thin decorative divider lines. Warm, appetising, elegant.

## 📱 QR Code — WhatsApp Direct Link

**Background colour to blend the QR code:** `#fcefe6` (cream)

**QR code colour:** `#324e2c` (forest green) — matches your brand

In Canva / QR code generator, set the QR background to `#fcefe6` so it has no white box and sits seamlessly on the pamphlet.

Copy and paste this link into any QR code generator (Canva Apps → QR Code, or QRCode Monkey, etc.):

```
https://wa.me/393758297864?text=Hi%20Alpha%20Tours!%20I%20saw%20your%20Food%20Tours%20pamphlet%20and%20I%27d%20love%20to%20know%20more%20about%20your%20food%20tours%3A%20pricing%20and%20availability.
```

When scanned, this will open WhatsApp directly with the following message pre-filled (in English):

> *"Hi Alpha Tours! I saw your Food Tours pamphlet and I'd love to know more about your food tours: pricing and availability."*

The client just needs to tap send — no typing required.

## 🎯 PROMPT 3 — DALL-E · "Eat Like a Roman" Badge Banner (for food tour pamphlet)

Design a horizontal banner / ribbon graphic (landscape orientation, ~3:1 ratio) that can be placed at the top or bottom of a print pamphlet promoting food tours.

**Visual:** A wide cinematic scene of a traditional Roman trattoria table at golden hour. The table is set with a red-and-white checkered tablecloth. On it: a steaming plate of cacio e pepe pasta, a bottle of Chianti wine, a basket of crusty bread, and small bowls of olive oil and balsamic. In the soft-focus background through a window: a view of Trastevere's terracotta rooftops at sunset. Warm, golden, welcoming light floods the scene.

**Overlay text (centred in the upper portion of the image, over a warm semi-transparent gold band):**

- **Heading line (large, bold):** "🇮🇹 EAT LIKE A ROMAN" — in a sophisticated serif font like Playfair Display, colour: forest green (#324e2c), with subtle shadow or glow for readability.
- **Subheading (smaller, directly below):** "Authentic Roman Cuisine · Guided Experiences" — in uppercase, same serif font, colour: dark charcoal (#1a1a1a).
- **Third line (smallest, below that):** "From Testaccio market to Trastevere's hidden gems" — in a clean sans-serif font like Inter, colour: deep red (#a71b20).

**Bottom edge of banner (thin strip):** A decorative line with tiny dots in amber/gold (#f5b35d) and forest green (#324e2c) alternating.

**Colour palette:** Cream/blush (#fcefe6) background accents, forest green (#324e2c), deep red (#a71b20), amber/gold (#f5b35d) — matching the pamphlet brand colours exactly. Warm, appetising, authentic Italian mood.
