# Golf Cart Tours — Pamphlet Prompts for DALL-E

Use these prompts in ChatGPT with DALL-E to generate visual examples, then recreate in Canva for print.

---

## 🎯 PROMPT 1 — FRONT COVER

Design a front cover of a print travel pamphlet / flyer (portrait A5 / DL format) for a luxury tour company called "Alpha Tours Rome" promoting "Golf Cart Tours of Rome".

**Visual:** A comfortable open-top electric golf cart in forest green, photographed from a low angle making it look large and prominent in the foreground. The cart takes up roughly the bottom third of the frame. In the background (soft focus / bokeh): The Colosseum at golden hour, warm sunlight streaming through umbrella pines. The golf cart seats are visible with 2-3 happy passengers smiling. The scene should feel joyful, easy, and carefree — the "no walking, no queues" experience.

**Title (top two-thirds, large):** "GOLF CART TOURS" in a sophisticated serif font like Playfair Display, colour: forest green (#324e2c).

**Subtitle directly below the title, smaller:** "Rome's Highlights Without the Walks" — same serif font, but in amber/gold (#f5b35d) with elegant letter-spacing.

**Bottom edge (over a narrow semi-transparent band):** Logo "Alpha Tours Rome" in small caps. Tagline: "Eco-Friendly · Up to 7 Guests · Private Tours" in tiny uppercase letters, dark charcoal.

**Colour palette (strict):** Cream/blush background (#fcefe6), forest green (#324e2c) for title, deep red (#a71b20) for tiny accent elements (decorative dots, lines), amber/gold (#f5b35d) for subtitle. Clean, premium, joyful mood.

## 🎯 PROMPT 2 — BACK COVER

Design the back cover of a print travel pamphlet / flyer (portrait A5 / DL format) for "Alpha Tours Rome" — a luxury tour company — promoting their golf cart tours.

**Visual (top half):** A soft collage with three warm vignette images fading into each other, all with golden-hour filter and soft blurred edges:
- Left panel: A golf cart parked at the Trevi Fountain with tourists taking photos in the background.
- Centre panel: Close-up of a guide pointing at the Pantheon dome while passengers in the golf cart look up in awe.
- Right panel: The view from a golf cart driving along a narrow cobblestone Roman street with terracotta buildings on both sides.

**Layout — bottom half (cream/ivory background):**

Section title "OUR GOLF CART TOURS" in forest green (#324e2c), serif font, centred.

Below, the tours listed in two clean columns (4 left, 3 right) as small icon + text lines:

🛺 **Rome Highlights Private Tour** — 2.5h · Private · ★ Most Popular

🏆 **Top Ten Major Attractions** — 2.5h · Up to 7 guests

💰 **Enjoy Major Attractions** — 2.5h · Up to 7 · ★ Great Value

**Very bottom centre:** Small "Alpha Tours Rome" logo.

**Colour palette (strict):** Cream background (#fcefe6), forest green (#324e2c) for section titles, deep red (#a71b20) for tiny decorative accents, dark charcoal (#1a1a1a) for body text, amber/gold (#f5b35d) for thin decorative divider lines. Minimalist, elegant, not crowded.

## 📱 QR Code — WhatsApp Direct Link

**Background colour to blend the QR code:** `#fcefe6` (cream)

**QR code colour:** `#324e2c` (forest green) — matches your brand

In Canva / QR code generator, set the QR background to `#fcefe6` so it has no white box and sits seamlessly on the pamphlet.

Copy and paste this link into any QR code generator (Canva Apps → QR Code, or QRCode Monkey, etc.):

```
https://wa.me/393758297864?text=Hi%20Alpha%20Tours!%20I%20saw%20your%20Golf%20Cart%20Tours%20pamphlet%20and%20I%27d%20love%20to%20know%20more%20about%20pricing%20and%20availability.
```

When scanned, this will open WhatsApp directly with the following message pre-filled (in English):

> *"Hi Alpha Tours! I saw your Golf Cart Tours pamphlet and I'd love to know more about pricing and availability."*

The client just needs to tap send — no typing required.

## 🎯 PROMPT 3 — DALL-E · "Most Popular" Badge Banner (for golf cart pamphlet)

Design a horizontal banner / ribbon graphic (landscape orientation, ~3:1 ratio) that can be placed at the top or bottom of a print pamphlet promoting golf cart tours.

**Visual:** A wide panoramic scene photographed from a low angle looking up at a forest green golf cart with the Colosseum in the background at sunset. The golf cart is parked on ancient Roman cobblestones. Warm golden light illuminates the scene creating long dramatic shadows. The composition should feel inviting, adventurous, and premium.

**Overlay text (centred in the upper portion of the image, over a warm semi-transparent gold band):**

- **Heading line (large, bold):** "🏆 AWARD-WINNING TOURS" — in a sophisticated serif font like Playfair Display, colour: forest green (#324e2c), with subtle shadow or glow for readability against the bright background.
- **Subheading (smaller, directly below):** "TripAdvisor Travelers' Choice 2017-2025" — in uppercase, same serif font, colour: dark charcoal (#1a1a1a).
- **Third line (smallest, below that):** "Book your private golf cart experience today" — in a clean sans-serif font like Inter, colour: deep red (#a71b20).

**Bottom edge of banner (thin strip):** A decorative line with tiny dots in amber/gold (#f5b35d) and forest green (#324e2c) alternating.

**Colour palette:** Cream/blush (#fcefe6) background accents, forest green (#324e2c), deep red (#a71b20), amber/gold (#f5b35d) — matching the pamphlet brand colours exactly. The banner should feel like a natural part of the pamphlet, not a separate sticker. Elegant, premium, award-winning mood.
