# FIAT 500 — Dolce Vita Experience — Pamphlet Prompts for DALL-E

Use these prompts in ChatGPT with DALL-E to generate visual examples, then recreate in Canva for print.

## 🎯 PROMPT 1 — FRONT COVER

Design a front cover of a print travel pamphlet / flyer (portrait A5 / DL format) for a luxury tour company called "Alpha Tours Rome" promoting the "FIAT 500 Dolce Vita Experience".

**Visual:** A vintage Fiat 500 car in pastel light blue, photographed from a low angle making it look large and prominent in the foreground. The car takes up roughly the bottom half of the frame. In the background (soft focus / bokeh): Circo Massimo and the Roseto Comunale rose gardens at golden hour, with the Palatine Hill visible. Two elegantly dressed people are standing next to the car — their heads reaching about the level of the car roof — holding small glasses of limoncello. The car-to-human proportion should feel natural and realistic, the car large and iconic.

**Title (top two-thirds, large):** "FIAT 500" in a sophisticated serif font like Playfair Display, colour: forest green (#324e2c).

**Subtitle directly below the title, smaller:** "Dolce Vita Experience" — same serif font, but in amber/gold (#f5b35d) with elegant letter-spacing.

**Bottom edge (over a narrow semi-transparent band):** Logo "Alpha Tours Rome" in small caps. Tagline: "Classic Italian Style · Dolce Vita · Drive Through History" in tiny uppercase letters, dark charcoal.

**Colour palette (strict):** Cream/blush background (#fcefe6), forest green (#324e2c) for title, deep red (#a71b20) for tiny accent elements (decorative dots, lines), amber/gold (#f5b35d) for subtitle. No "4-Hour" or "4h" text anywhere — keep it elegant and minimal. Clean, premium, Dolce Vita mood.

## 🎯 PROMPT 2 — BACK COVER

Design the back cover of a print travel pamphlet / flyer (portrait A5 / DL format) for "Alpha Tours Rome" — a luxury tour company — promoting the "FIAT 500 Dolce Vita Experience".

**Visual (top half):** A soft collage with three warm vignette images fading into each other, all with golden-hour filter and soft blurred edges:

- Centre panel: Close-up of a Polaroid photo — someone holding strawberries and a glass of prosecco at a fruit stand.
- Right panel: A plate of traditional Roman pasta at a restaurant table (Taste Accio in Testaccio).

**Layout — bottom half (cream/ivory background):**

Section title "THE JOURNEY" in forest green (#324e2c), serif font, centred.

Below, 8 itinerary stops listed in two clean columns (4 left, 4 right) as small icon + text lines:

🍋 Circo Massimo — Limoncello & Photos

🔭 St. Peter's Optical Illusion

**Left bottom corner:** Contact info: "📍 Circo Massimo · ⏱ 4 hours · 📞 +39 375 829 7864" in tiny dark charcoal text.

**Colour palette (strict):** Cream background (#fcefe6), forest green (#324e2c) for section titles, deep red (#a71b20) for tiny decorative accents, dark charcoal (#1a1a1a) for body text, amber/gold (#f5b35d) for thin decorative divider lines. Minimalist, elegant, not crowded.

## 🎯 PROMPT 3 — DALL-E · 20% Discount Banner (for combined Fiat 500 pamphlet)
