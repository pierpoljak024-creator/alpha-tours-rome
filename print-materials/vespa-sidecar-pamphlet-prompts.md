## 🎯 PROMPT 1 — FRONT COVER

Design a front cover of a print travel pamphlet / flyer (portrait A5 / DL format) for a luxury tour company called "Alpha Tours Rome" promoting "Vespa Sidecar Tours of Rome".

**Visual:** A vintage Vespa scooter in classic cream/white with a matching sidecar, photographed from a low side angle making it look stylish and romantic. The Vespa takes up roughly the bottom third of the frame. In the background (soft focus / bokeh): The Colosseum at golden hour, with warm sunlight filtering through umbrella pines. A stylish couple (elegant scarf, sunglasses) are about to get into the sidecar — the woman has her scarf blowing in the wind, the man wears a classic Italian style. The scene should feel cinematic, romantic, and utterly Roman — "La Dolce Vita" feeling.

**Title (top half, large):** "VESPA SIDECAR" in a sophisticated serif font like Playfair Display, colour: forest green (#324e2c).

**Subtitle directly below the title, smaller:** "Express Highlights of Rome" — same serif font, but in amber/gold (#f5b35d) with elegant letter-spacing.

**Tag below subtitle, even smaller:** "The Most Stylish Way to Explore Rome" in tiny uppercase, deep red (#a71b20).

**Bottom edge (over a narrow semi-transparent band):** Logo "Alpha Tours Rome" in small caps. Tagline: "Romantic · Unique · Perfect for Photos" in tiny uppercase letters, dark charcoal.

**Colour palette (strict):** Cream/blush background (#fcefe6), forest green (#324e2c) for title, deep red (#a71b20) for accent, amber/gold (#f5b35d) for subtitle. Clean, romantic, vintage Italian mood.

## 📱 QR Code — WhatsApp Direct Link

**Background colour to blend the QR code:** `#fcefe6` (cream)

**QR code colour:** `#324e2c` (forest green) — matches your brand

In Canva / QR code generator, set the QR background to `#fcefe6` so it has no white box and sits seamlessly on the pamphlet.

Copy and paste this link into any QR code generator (Canva Apps → QR Code, or QRCode Monkey, etc.):

```
https://wa.me/393758297864?text=Hi%20Alpha%20Tours!%20I%20saw%20your%20Vespa%20Sidecar%20pamphlet%20and%20I%27d%20love%20to%20know%20more%20about%20pricing%20and%20availability%20for%20the%20Vespa%20tours.
```

When scanned, this will open WhatsApp directly with the following message pre-filled (in English):

> *"Hi Alpha Tours! I saw your Vespa Sidecar pamphlet and I'd love to know more about pricing and availability for the Vespa tours."*

The client just needs to tap send — no typing required.
