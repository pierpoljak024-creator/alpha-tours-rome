## Executive Summary

Alpha Tours Rome is a boutique, private-tour operator with 20+ years of local expertise and a strong premium brand identity. The business offers golf cart tours, Vespa sidecar tours, FIAT 500 vintage tours, food tours, private day trips, and airport transfers.

The current opportunity is significant: the GYG listing sits at a perfect 5/5 from 7 reviews — an exceptional quality signal — but the review volume (7) vs. market leader City Walkers Tours (1,554) is the single biggest gap to close. The price premium (€53 vs competitor €39) is defensible given the private/exclusive positioning, but that differentiation must be communicated clearly and consistently across all channels.

The proposals below are grouped into six strategic pillars:

1. OTA Optimisation & Review Velocity
2. Website SEO & Content
3. Social Media Growth
4. New Products & Experiences
5. Pricing & Conversion
6. B2B & Partnership Channels

## Pillar 1 — OTA Optimisation & Review Velocity

### 1.1 Review Generation Campaign (Priority: Critical)

The 7-review count is the most urgent bottleneck. Every booking must trigger a structured review ask.

**Proposal: WhatsApp Post-Tour Follow-Up Flow**
- 2 hours after tour end, the guide sends a personalised WhatsApp message
- Include: short thank-you, guide name, one photo taken during the tour, direct GYG/Viator review link
- Script: *"Hi [name], it was a pleasure showing you Rome today! If you enjoyed it, a short review on [platform] would mean the world to us — it only takes a minute: [link]. Grazie mille! — [Guide name]"*
- Target: 1 review per 3 bookings (33% conversion) → with 10 bookings/week, +15 reviews/month
- **90-day goal:** 50+ reviews, closing the credibility gap on GYG

**Proposal: QR Review Card**
- Print a small credit-card-sized card handed to guests at tour end
- Front: Alpha Tours logo + *"Loved your tour? Tell the world!"*
- Back: QR codes to GYG, Viator, TripAdvisor, and Google
- Cost: negligible (printable in-house or local print shop)

**Proposal: Google Business Profile Reviews**
- Add Google review link alongside GYG in the WhatsApp flow
- Google reviews directly feed local SEO — these compound over time

### 1.2 Viator & TripAdvisor Listings

The GYG listing is live with a perfect score. Viator (owned by TripAdvisor) is the dominant OTA for the US market and essential for full coverage.

**Proposal: Launch Viator Listings**
- Create listings for: Golf Cart Tour, FIAT 500 Tour, Vespa Sidecar Tour, Food Tour
- Use the same premium photography and copy, adapted for Viator format
- Emphasise "private tour" explicitly — Viator users actively filter for private experiences
- Price at GYG parity (Viator takes 20–25% commission; build this into the price)
- Once 10+ GYG reviews exist, reference in Viator descriptions: *"Rated 5/5 on GetYourGuide"

**Proposal: Optimise Existing GYG Listing**
- Add photos to all available carousel slots (more photos = higher CTR)
- Add a short video (15–30 seconds of golf cart gliding past the Colosseum at golden hour)
- List included food stops prominently — the competitor listing mentions gelato in the title and highlights
- Move dietary restriction info to a more visible location (currently buried in fine print)

### 1.3 Bokun Channel Manager

With the Bokun account already configured, ensure all OTA channels sync availability through Bokun as volume grows — avoiding double-bookings is critical once Viator, GYG, and direct are running simultaneously.

Rome's visual character makes Instagram the primary social channel. Happy guests + iconic backdrops = highly shareable content.

**Proposed Weekly Content Mix:**

| Type | Frequency | Example |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Tour moment photo | 2x/week | Golf cart at Colosseum, golden hour |
| Reel / short video | 1x/week | 15–30 sec tour highlights with trending audio |

| Guest feature / review | 1x/week | Quote from a 5-star review + matching photo |

- Feature named guides (Claudio, Gabriele) — reviews mention them by name, which builds direct trust

- Ask guests at tour end to tag @alphatoursrome in their Rome photos

## Pillar 4 — New Products & Experiences

### 4.1 Themed & Seasonal Tours

**Proposal: Sunset / Evening Rome Tour**
- Golden hour and evening atmosphere is Rome at its most photogenic
- Reposition or add a premium evening variant of the golf cart tour (+€15–20)
- Name: *"Rome After Dark: The Eternal City at Golden Hour"*
- Strong photo opportunities → high shareability → organic marketing effect

**Proposal: Honeymoon & Anniversary Package**
- Combine FIAT 500 tour + aperitivo stop + private food experience
- Price as a premium bundle: €150–250/couple
- Target: newly engaged couples, anniversary travellers (high spend, high review rate)
- Keywords: *"romantic tour rome"*, *"rome honeymoon experience"*

**Proposal: Family Adventure Tour**
- Tailored commentary for children (mythology, gladiators, "gross" Roman history they love)
- Longer stops at kid-friendly sites: Circus Maximus, Colosseum exterior
- Multiple reviews already mention teenagers — formalise and market this
- Name: *"Little Gladiators: Rome Family Adventure"*

**Proposal: Photography Tour Add-On**
- Guests pay a small premium (+€20) for a photo stop package
- Guide positions the golf cart at optimal angles for iconic shots
- Could include 5–10 phone portraits taken by the guide at prime spots
- Outcome: guests get great photos → share online → organic brand reach

### 4.2 Private Day Trips (Product Formalisation)

**Proposal: Ostia Antica Day Trip**
- Less crowded alternative to Pompeii, 30 min from Rome by car
- Full-day private guided tour in FIAT 500 or minivan
- Name: *"Ancient Rome's Lost City: Private Ostia Antica Tour"*

**Proposal: Castelli Romani Wine & Villages Tour**
- Half-day trip to the Alban Hills wine country
- 2–3 cantina visits, local lunch, private guide
- Premium pricing (€200+/couple) — high-margin product

**Proposal: Corporate & Incentive Team Experiences**
- Small corporate groups (8–20 pax) in multiple golf carts
- *"Team Rome: Discover the Eternal City Together"*
- Higher per-head revenue, fills weekday slots (off-peak for leisure)
- Target: incentive travel agencies, HR departments, conference organisers

## Pillar 5 — Pricing & Conversion

### 5.1 Current Pricing Analysis

| Tour | Alpha Current | Competitor (City Walkers) | Difference |
|------|--------------|--------------------------|------------|
| Golf Cart Tour | €53/person | €39/person (shared) | +€14 |

The premium is **fully defensible** given Alpha's private model — but the listing descriptions must make this explicit.

**Proposal: Private vs. Shared Messaging**
- Add to every OTA listing: *"Unlike shared tours, your cart is exclusively yours — no strangers, no compromises, just your group and your guide."*
- Create a comparison table on the website showing Alpha private vs. shared tour experience

### 5.2 Pricing Structure Proposal

Consider moving from per-person to per-cart pricing for couples and small groups:

| Booking Type | Current | Proposed |
|--------------|---------|----------|
| Solo | €53 | €59 (private premium) |
| Couple (2 pax) | €106 total | €89/cart (better value perception) |
| Group 3 pax | €159 total | €120/cart (€40/pp) |
| Group 4 pax | €212 total | €149/cart (€37/pp) |

This makes the couple booking significantly more attractive while protecting margin on solo bookings.

### 5.3 Direct Booking Incentive

**Proposal: 10% Direct Booking Discount**
- OTAs charge 20–25% commission; passing some saving to direct bookers improves margin and builds loyalty
- Promote via: Instagram bio link, WhatsApp follow-up, email footer
- *"Book direct and save 10% — WhatsApp: +39 375 829 7864"*

### 5.4 Last-Minute Availability Deals

**Proposal: Same-Day Flash Offers**
- If a slot is unsold by 9am that morning, push a story/WhatsApp offer: *"Today only — 15% off our 3pm golf cart tour. DM us to book!"*
- Fills otherwise empty slots, cultivates direct-booking habits

## Pillar 6 — B2B & Partnership Channels

### 6.1 Hotel Concierge Programme

**Proposal: Concierge Referral Commission**
- Rome's 4–5★ hotels have concierge desks recommending daily activities to guests
- Offer a 10–15% referral commission per completed booking
- Provide concierges: printed brochures, a simple rate card, and a dedicated WhatsApp booking line
- Priority hotels: those near tour start points (Anantara Naiadi, Marriott, NH Hotels near Piazza della Repubblica)

### 6.2 Civitatis (Spanish-Speaking Market)

**Proposal: List on Civitatis**
- Civitatis dominates the Spanish and Latin American travel market
- Rome is consistently their #1 destination by volume
- Alpha Tours' warm, personal style maps well to this audience
- Estimated incremental bookings once established: 5–10/month

### 6.3 Cruise Ship Excursion Product

**Proposal: Civitavecchia Port Pickup Package**
- Many Rome visitors arrive by cruise from Civitavecchia (90 min from Rome)
- Offer: port pickup → Rome highlights golf cart tour → drop-off
- Price at cruise-excursion levels: €120–180/person — premium justified by full-day convenience
- Market through shore excursion forums, cruise Facebook groups, and direct outreach to cruise lines

### 6.4 B2B Travel Agency Net Rates

**Proposal: Trade Rate Sheet for UK/US Travel Agents**
- Many UK and US travellers book Rome through travel agents who package itineraries
- Prepare a simple net rate sheet: 25% off retail, minimum 10 bookings/month guarantee
- Submit profiles to: TourRadar (supplier side), Civitatis, Hotelbeds
